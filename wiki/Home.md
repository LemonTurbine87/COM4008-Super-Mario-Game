# Project Wiki – Super Mario (COM4008 CW1)

This wiki documents the design, development, and testing of my solution for COM4008 CW1.

## Pages
- [Development Log](Development-Log) – entries of what was built and why
- [Testing](Testing) – how each requirement was tested, and results
- [Concepts Explained](Concepts-Explained) – notes for the demonstration/explanation component

## How to use this wiki (for me, going forward)
- Update **Development Log** roughly every session, even a few lines — markers want to see *reasoning over time*, not just a final commit.
- Update **Testing** whenever I've manually tested a feature or have fixed a bug - what I've tried, what happened when I have and then what I changed.
- Keep **Concepts Explained** updated as I go so that they're explained within my presentation.
